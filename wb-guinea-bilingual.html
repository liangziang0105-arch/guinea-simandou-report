前言 Preface

Guinea's economy delivered strong performance in 2025, with growth accelerating and inflation declining significantly. Yet, as in many resource-rich economies, this strong macroeconomic performance has not fully translated into job creation or broad-based improvements in living standards.

几内亚经济在 2025 年表现强劲，增长加速、通胀显著回落。然而，与许多资源富集型经济体一样，这一强劲的宏观表现并未充分转化为就业创造或民生水平的普遍改善。

Guinea now stands at a pivotal moment. The ramp up of iron ore production from Simandou is set to propel the country into a period of rapid growth, creating a historic opportunity to accelerate development. At the same time, this expansion brings important domestic macroeconomic risks, particularly pressures on competitiveness associated with large mining inflows. In parallel, the global environment has become more uncertain, including due to the conflict in the Middle East.

几内亚正处于关键的历史节点。西芒杜铁矿石产能的爬坡将推动该国进入高速增长期，创造了加速发展的历史性机遇。与此同时，这一扩张也带来了不容忽视的国内宏观风险，尤其是大规模矿业资金流入带来的竞争力压力（即"荷兰病"风险）。此外，全球环境不确定性上升，包括中东冲突的影响。

The central challenge is clear: to transform resource-driven growth into productive and inclusive jobs. Mining, while a key engine of growth and revenue, is capital-intensive and generates relatively few jobs. At the same time, Guinea's rapidly growing and youthful population is entering the labor market in large numbers, making job creation essential for sustained poverty reduction and shared prosperity.

核心挑战十分明确：必须把资源驱动的增长转化为生产性的、包容性的就业。矿业虽是增长和财政收入的关键引擎，但属于资本密集型产业，创造的就业相对有限。与此同时，几内亚快速增长的年轻人口正大量进入劳动力市场，使就业创造成为持续减贫和共享繁荣的必要条件。

This report emphasizes the need to strengthen the non-mining private sector as the main driver of employment. It highlights the potential of agriculture, agribusiness, and services to generate jobs at scale, and the importance of fostering stronger linkages between mining and domestic firms through effective local content policies and supplier development.

本报告强调需要壮大非矿业私营部门，使其成为就业的主要引擎。报告重点分析了农业、涉农产业和服务业规模化创造就业的潜力，以及通过有效的本地化政策和供应商发展计划，加强矿业与本土企业之间产业联动的重要性。

To support this transformation, the report highlights that Guinea could draw on the World Bank Group's Jobs Framework, which emphasizes three essential foundations—foundational infrastructure, sound governance, and private capital mobilization—alongside five sectors with strong job creation potential, and World Bank Group strategic priorities. These priorities are well aligned with Guinea's development strategy, including the Simandou 2040 program.

为支持这一转型，报告指出几内亚可借鉴世界银行集团的"就业框架"——该框架强调三大基础（基础性基础设施、良善治理、私人资本动员）以及五个具有强劲就业创造潜力的部门。这些优先事项与几内亚自身的发展战略高度契合，包括"西芒杜 2040"计划。

The outlook for Guinea is promising, but the path forward requires decisive action. Sustaining macroeconomic stability, strengthening public financial management, and advancing reforms that support private sector development will be critical to translating growth into tangible gains for the population.

几内亚前景可期，但前路需要果断行动。维持宏观经济稳定、加强公共财政管理、推进支持私营部门发展的改革，是把增长转化为民众切实收益的关键。

The World Bank Group remains a committed partner to Guinea in this effort—supporting reforms, mobilizing financing, and fostering opportunities for inclusive growth. A more prosperous future is within reach—if today's growth is turned into quality jobs for all Guineans.

世界银行集团将继续作为几内亚的坚定伙伴——支持改革、动员融资、培育包容性增长的机会。只要将今天的增长转化为全体几内亚人的优质就业，更加繁荣的未来就触手可及。

——Issa Diaw，世界银行集团几内亚国别经理

执行摘要 Executive Summary

强劲的经济表现与持续的脆弱性并存 A strong economic performance, with persistent fragilities

Guinea's economy accelerated sharply in 2025, delivering its strongest performance in years, yet the gains remain unequally shared. Real GDP grew by 7.4 percent (4.8 percent per capita), driven by a 13.9 percent expansion in mining—fueled by a 30 percent surge in bauxite output—and supported by a broad-based recovery in services and a 5.8 percent expansion in agriculture. Inflation fell sharply, from 5.1 percent in 2024 to 3.2 percent in 2025, as the effects of the December 2023 fuel depot explosion faded and transport prices declined. The acceleration of real GDP growth alongside disinflation has supported real household purchasing power and the national poverty rate is projected to have fallen from 43.3 percent in 2024 to 37.8 percent in 2025. However, the link from economic growth to welfare improvement remained weak in recent years: despite GDP growth averaging 6.5 percent between 2016 and 2024, poverty increased between 2019 and 2022, pushing an estimated 1.2 million additional people below the national poverty line—a reminder that aggregate growth, on its own, is not enough.

几内亚经济在 2025 年大幅提速，取得多年来最强表现，但增长成果的分配并不均衡。实际 GDP 增长 7.4%（人均 4.8%），驱动力来自矿业 13.9% 的扩张（铝土矿产量激增 30%），并得益于服务业广泛复苏和农业 5.8% 的增长。通胀率从 2024 年的 5.1% 大幅降至 2025 年的 3.2%，原因是 2023 年 12 月油库爆炸的冲击消退、运输价格回落。实际 GDP 加速增长叠加通胀回落，支撑了家庭实际购买力，全国贫困率预计从 2024 年的 43.3% 降至 2025 年的 37.8%。然而近年来增长与民生改善之间的传导仍然薄弱：尽管 2016–2024 年 GDP 年均增长 6.5%，2019–2022 年贫困反而上升，估计新增 120 万人跌至国家贫困线以下——这提醒我们，仅靠总量增长是不够的。

This third edition of the Guinea Economic Update reviews the country's macroeconomic performance in 2025 and assesses the prospects and risks ahead. It then turns to a question of central importance: how can Guinea harness its mining boom to create more and better jobs through private sector development and economic diversification? The report's findings are both encouraging and cautionary—encouraging because Guinea's near-term growth outlook is among the strongest in the world; cautionary because the macroeconomic and structural constraints hindering that growth from reaching ordinary Guineans requires firm implementation of wide-ranging and coherent reforms, also discussed in this report.

本期（第三版）《几内亚经济更新》回顾了该国 2025 年的宏观经济表现，评估了前景与风险，随后聚焦一个核心问题：几内亚如何通过私营部门发展和经济多元化，把矿业繁荣转化为更多、更好的就业？报告的结论既鼓舞人心又发出警示——鼓舞在于几内亚近期增长前景位居全球最强之列；警示在于，要让增长惠及普通几内亚人，必须坚定实施广泛而协调的改革（本报告对此有详细讨论），以克服宏观与结构性约束。

Macroeconomic management was mixed in 2025, with strong revenue performance offset by a sharp increase in spending, while financial sector vulnerabilities and rising domestic debt warrant close attention. On the fiscal side, a landmark revenue effort raised total revenues by 3 percentage points to 18.2 percent of GDP—the highest in over 20 years—driven by stronger tax administration, digitalization of customs procedures, and improved mining revenue collection. However, a surge in capital investment and election-related outlays pushed total expenditure to 27.1 percent of GDP, causing the fiscal deficit to widen sharply to 8.9 percent of GDP from 4.9 percent in 2024. As a result, public debt rose to 51.5 percent of GDP, driven almost entirely by domestic borrowing, which is a growing vulnerability as it carries higher interest costs and shorter maturities than concessional external financing. In the financial sector, monetary policy easing brought the policy rate to 9.75 percent, but credit transmission remained weak: private sector credit stood at just 10.2 percent of GDP. A cash shortage imposed real costs on firms and households as bank vault holdings contracted by 16.5 percent year-on-year despite available electronic liquidity. Gross international reserves rebounded sharply, from 1.4 months of import cover at end-2024 to 3.6 months by end-2025, restoring an important buffer.

2025 年的宏观管理喜忧参半：财政收入表现强劲，但支出大幅攀升，金融部门脆弱性与国内债务上升值得高度关注。财政方面，历史性的创收努力使财政总收入占 GDP 比重提高 3 个百分点至 18.2%——为 20 多年来最高水平——驱动力包括税收征管强化、海关程序数字化和矿业收入征收改善。然而，资本性投资和选举相关支出激增，使总支出达到 GDP 的 27.1%，财政赤字从 2024 年的 4.9% 急剧扩大至 8.9%。公共债务随之升至 GDP 的 51.5%，几乎全部由国内借款驱动——国内借款利率更高、期限更短，比优惠性外部融资更具脆弱性。金融部门方面，货币政策宽松使政策利率降至 9.75%，但信贷传导依然疲弱：私营部门信贷仅占 GDP 的 10.2%。尽管电子流动性充足，银行库存现金同比收缩 16.5%，现金短缺给企业和家庭带来真实成本。国际储备总额大幅回升，从 2024 年底的 1.4 个月进口覆盖升至 2025 年底的 3.6 个月，重建了重要缓冲。

Guinea's medium-term outlook is strong, driven by rising Simandou production, but risks are tilted to the downside and must be managed proactively. Simandou iron ore exports, commenced in late 2025, will propel GDP growth to an estimated 8.8 percent in 2026 and 11.6 percent in 2027—among the highest rates in the world. Poverty rates are expected to decline further as real incomes rise. However, this favorable baseline is subject to meaningful risks. The ongoing conflict in the Middle East is expected to affect Guinea primarily through higher oil and shipping costs and a slowdown in global growth. While higher gold prices may provide a partial buffer to export revenues, the net effect is likely to be negative, increasing inflationary pressures, raising import and production costs, including in the mining sector, and eroding fiscal space. Dutch disease pressures—whereby rapid mining export growth appreciates the real effective exchange rate and erodes competitiveness of tradable agriculture, manufacturing, and services—represent a macroeconomic risk that could undermine the very diversification Guinea needs.

几内亚中期前景强劲，由西芒杜产量上升驱动，但风险偏向下行，必须主动管理。2025 年底启动的西芒杜铁矿石出口将推动 GDP 增速在 2026 年达到约 8.8%、2027 年达 11.6%——位居全球最高之列。随着实际收入上升，贫困率预计进一步下降。但这一乐观基线面临实质风险：中东冲突预计主要通过油价与海运成本上升、全球增长放缓传导至几内亚；金价走高虽可部分缓冲出口收入，但净效应可能为负——推高通胀压力、抬升包括矿业在内的进口与生产成本、侵蚀财政空间。荷兰病压力（矿业出口快速增长推升实际有效汇率、削弱可贸易的农业、制造业和服务业竞争力）是一种宏观风险，可能恰恰破坏几内亚所需要的多元化。

Guinea's long-term growth could range from a sluggish 5.1 percent under a business-as-usual path to nearly 6.9 percent under a reform scenario by 2040, with the difference determined not by Simandou itself but by the policy choices Guinea makes in the years ahead. Under the baseline, double-digit growth between 2027 and 2028 masks a narrowly based expansion: structural constraints persist, Dutch disease dynamics suppress tradable non-mining sector competitiveness, and growth decelerates without meaningful job creation or improvements in living standards. A reform scenario tells a markedly different story—by 2040, growth could reach nearly 6.9 percent, some 1.8 percentage points above the baseline, driven by stronger human and physical capital, digitalization, and a more competitive business environment. These reforms would expand manufacturing and services, raise wages, increase social spending, and lift real GDP per capita 20 percent above the business-as-usual path.

几内亚长期增速到 2040 年可能在"照旧情景"下仅 5.1%、在"改革情景"下接近 6.9% 之间——差异不取决于西芒杜本身，而取决于几内亚未来几年的政策选择。基线情景下，2027–2028 年的两位数增长掩盖了狭窄的扩张基础：结构性约束依旧，荷兰病压制非矿可贸易部门竞争力，增长随后减速，就业和民生无明显改善。改革情景则截然不同：到 2040 年增速接近 6.9%、比基线高约 1.8 个百分点，驱动力来自更强的人力与物质资本、数字化和更具竞争力的营商环境，制造业和服务业扩张、工资上升、社会支出增加，人均实际 GDP 比照旧路径高出 20%。

结构性挑战：增长未能带来足够就业 The structural challenge: Growth without enough jobs

Guinea's first mining boom demonstrated a difficult truth: growth driven by capital-intensive extraction rarely creates enough jobs to reduce poverty at scale. Mining generates foreign exchange and fiscal revenues, but its direct employment footprint is small and the real effective exchange rate appreciation it induces erodes the competitiveness of tradable non-mining sectors. The result is that employment in Guinea offers surprisingly little protection against poverty. In the latest national living standard survey (EHCVM 2018-19), poverty rates among households with an employed head (43.9 percent) are barely lower than those with an unemployed head (42.0 percent). Over 81 percent of Guineans rely on self-employment or family enterprises, where poverty incidence approaches 48.8 percent, compared to 25.5 percent among households headed by wage employees. The central challenge is therefore not just more jobs, but better jobs: formal, productive, and adequately remunerated.

几内亚第一轮矿业繁荣证明了一个严峻事实：资本密集型采掘驱动的增长很少能创造足以大规模减贫的就业。矿业带来外汇和财政收入，但直接就业足迹很小，其引致的实际汇率升值还会侵蚀非矿可贸易部门的竞争力。结果是，在几内亚"有工作"对脱贫的保障出人意料地弱：最新全国生活水平调查（EHCVM 2018-19）显示，户主有工作的家庭贫困率（43.9%）几乎不低于户主失业的家庭（42.0%）。超过 81% 的几内亚人依赖自雇或家庭经营，其贫困发生率接近 48.8%，而户主为工资雇员的家庭仅 25.5%。因此核心挑战不只是"更多就业"，而是"更好的就业"——正规、有生产率、报酬合理的岗位。

The labor market is characterized by high informality, low productivity, and deep gender inequalities. Formal wage employment accounts for only 8.5 percent of the workforce, and approximately 92 percent of private businesses are micro-enterprises. Women face compounding disadvantages: their labor force participation rate (43 percent) trails men's (63 percent), over 95 percent of female employment is classified as vulnerable, and women spend nearly three times as many hours as men on unpaid care work. Poverty gaps by gender are stark—among adults aged 25–34, the poverty rate reaches 43 percent for women compared to 23 percent for men. One in three young Guineans aged 15–24 is not in education, employment, or training, rising to one in two among rural girls.

劳动力市场以高非正规性、低生产率和深层性别不平等为特征。正规工资就业仅占劳动力的 8.5%，约 92% 的私营企业是微型企业。女性面临叠加劣势：劳动参与率 43%（男性 63%），超过 95% 的女性就业属于脆弱就业，女性无酬照护劳动时间接近男性的三倍。25–34 岁人群中女性贫困率 43%、男性 23%。15–24 岁几内亚青年中三分之一处于"非在学、非就业、非培训"（NEET）状态，农村女孩中这一比例达二分之一。

西芒杜机遇——以及把握机遇所需的条件 The Simandou opportunity—and what it will take to seize it

The non-mining private sector shows latent dynamism, notwithstanding the macroeconomic risks, but is held back by infrastructure deficits, a burdensome business environment, and weak access to finance. Firms surveyed in the World Bank Enterprise Survey reported above SSA average sales and employment growth, signaling real underlying potential. Yet they identified transport and logistics as the top obstacle to expansion—cited at roughly three times the Sub-Saharan African average—followed by customs burdens and political instability. Access to finance remains structurally limited: high lending rates and banks' preference for government securities over private lending restrict credit to small and medium firms. Informality is pervasive, and 79 percent of WBES surveyed firms reported facing competition from informal firms although fewer than 10 percent viewed this as their primary obstacle.

非矿私营部门展现出潜在活力，但受制于基础设施缺口、繁重的营商环境和融资不畅。世界银行企业调查（WBES）中的正规企业销售与就业增长高于撒哈拉以南非洲平均水平，显示真实潜力。但企业把运输与物流列为扩张的首要障碍（提及率约为撒南非洲平均的 3 倍），其次是海关负担和政治不稳定。融资在结构性上受限：高贷款利率、银行偏好国债而非私人放贷，挤压了中小企业信贷。非正规经济普遍，79% 的受访企业称面临非正规企业竞争（但只有不到 10% 视其为首要障碍）。

The Simandou iron ore operations offers a historic—but not automatic—opportunity to break from Guinea's enclave growth model. The Government's Simandou 2040 program provides a strategic framework to channel resources into infrastructure, human capital (through skills development and education), agriculture value chains, and private non-mining sector development, envisioning approximately US$200 billion in investment from 2026 through 2040. The Bourse de sous-traitance et de partenariat (BSTP) has already channeled over US$236 million in mining procurement to local firms, representing meaningful institutional progress. Yet the mine is projected to create only 15,000–18,000 direct and indirect jobs—a start, but still a fraction of what Guinea's labor market needs. Local content program is a potential incubator for developing local non-mining firms. Realizing Simandou's broader employment potential will require scaling capacity building support to enable local firms to compete for mining sector contracts, expanding affordable finance, strengthening supplier development programs, and updating local content policy.

西芒杜铁矿运营提供了打破几内亚"飞地式增长"模式的历史性——但并非自动的——机遇。政府的"西芒杜 2040"计划提供了战略框架，拟在 2026–2040 年引导约 2000 亿美元投资进入基础设施、人力资本（技能与教育）、农业价值链和非矿私营部门。分包与伙伴关系交易所（BSTP）已向本地企业引导逾 2.36 亿美元矿业采购，是有意义的制度进展。然而矿山预计仅创造 1.5–1.8 万个直接与间接岗位——是开始，但只是劳动力市场所需的一小部分。本地化计划可成为培育本地非矿企业的孵化器。要实现西芒杜更广泛的就业潜力，需扩大对本地企业的能力建设支持、拓宽可负担融资、强化供应商发展计划并更新本地化政策。

Agriculture value chains—Guinea's largest employer and the sector most directly linked to rural poverty—must be at the center of any inclusive growth strategy. Over 60 percent of Guineans work in agriculture, yet the sector is constrained by weak productivity growth, significant post-harvest losses, and weak market connectivity. The Simandou rail-port corridor, if opened to multi-user access on equitable terms, could be transformative: reducing logistics costs, connecting smallholders to urban and export markets, and enabling agri-SMEs to supply food and services to mining operations. Niche value chains in fonio, mango, cashew, pineapple, and fish products already show genuine export potential.

农业价值链——几内亚最大的就业部门、与农村贫困联系最直接的部门——必须居于任何包容性增长战略的中心。超过 60% 的几内亚人从事农业，但该部门受制于生产率增长乏力、严重的采后损失和薄弱的市场连接。西芒杜铁路—港口走廊若以公平条件向多用户开放，可能带来变革：降低物流成本、把小农户连接到城市与出口市场、使农业中小企业能够为矿山供应食品与服务。福尼奥米、芒果、腰果、菠萝和水产品等利基价值链已展现真实的出口潜力。

Translating Guinea's mineral wealth into shared prosperity ultimately depends on a coherent reform agenda—and on acting now, before the window closes. Management of Simandou revenues must be anchored within a robust medium-term fiscal framework with guardrails for spending. A sovereign wealth fund being designed by the government should be governed by clear, transparent rules. Domestic revenue mobilization must continue through transfer pricing reform, deeper digitalization, and progressive reduction of energy subsidies that disproportionately benefit wealthier households. Human capital investment—particularly in girls' education, market-relevant skills training, and affordable childcare—is essential. Without decisive action, Guinea risks repeating the experience of its first mining boom—strong aggregate growth, weak poverty reduction, and a missed opportunity for structural transformation.

把矿产财富转化为共享繁荣，最终取决于一套协调的改革议程——以及立即行动，趁窗口尚未关闭。西芒杜收入的管理必须锚定在带有支出护栏的稳健中期财政框架内；政府正在设计的主权财富基金应有清晰透明的治理规则；国内创收须通过转让定价改革、深化数字化、逐步削减不成比例惠及富裕家庭的能源补贴来推进；人力资本投资——特别是女童教育、面向市场的技能培训与可负担的托育——不可或缺。若不果断行动，几内亚可能重蹈第一轮矿业繁荣的覆辙——总量增长强劲、减贫乏力、错失结构转型机遇。

第一章 经济表现、前景与政策优先事项 Chapter 1. Economic Performance, Outlook, and Policy Priorities

**本章要点 Overview：**几内亚经济在 2025 年展现韧性，铝土矿产量激增与服务业复苏推动 GDP 增速升至 7.4%，通胀大幅回落为货币宽松创造空间。贫困率明显下降，但荷兰病相关的宏观逆风与结构性障碍——资本密集型的矿业、高非正规性、人口快速增长、金融中介薄弱——可能限制增长向民生的转化。几内亚近 80% 人口在 35 岁以下，正规工资就业仅占劳动力 8.5%。尽管创收强劲，选举支出与资本投资扩张使财政状况显著恶化；西芒杜相关进口激增使外部压力持续。展望未来，西芒杜铁矿石出口将把几内亚推入数年的高增长期，但要实现包容性发展，必须在财政管理、定向公共投资和私营部门发展上果断改革。

1.1 近期经济与贫困动态 Recent Economic and Poverty Developments

1.1.1 全球增长平淡，撒南非洲回升但对就业拉动有限

Global economic growth held steady in 2025. According to the June 2026 Global Economic Prospects, global output expanded by 2.9 percent in 2025, unchanged from 2024. While inflationary pressures continued to ease, trade policy uncertainty continued to weigh on business confidence and investment.

2025 年全球经济增长持平。据 2026 年 6 月《全球经济展望》，全球产出 2025 年增长 2.9%，与 2024 年持平，在投资走弱、财政偏紧、贸易摩擦持续的背景下表现出韧性。通胀压力继续缓和使货币政策逐步宽松，但贸易政策不确定性继续压制企业信心与投资，尤其是深度融入全球供应链的行业。

Sub-Saharan Africa (SSA) experienced stronger-than-expected growth in 2025. Regional growth rose to 4.1 percent, up from 3.8 percent in 2024, supported by improved inflation management, ongoing fiscal consolidation, and a favorable external environment. The median inflation rate decelerated from 4.4 percent to 3.7 percent. Primary deficits narrowed sharply, from a peak of 3.2 percent of GDP in 2020 to an estimated 0.7 percent in 2025. However, growth outcomes remain uneven and insufficient to translate into meaningful job creation—a dynamic that resonates with Guinea's own experience. Per capita growth reached only 1.6 percent in 2025.

撒哈拉以南非洲 2025 年增长超预期：区域增速从 2024 年的 3.8% 升至 4.1%，受益于通胀管理改善、财政整固持续推进和有利的外部环境（美元走弱、主要出口商品价格高企）。区域通胀中位数从 4.4% 降至 3.7%，约七成经济体价格压力放缓。初级赤字从 2020 年占 GDP 3.2% 的峰值收窄至 2025 年约 0.7%。但各国增长不均，且不足以转化为有意义的就业创造——这一点与几内亚自身经历相呼应：2025 年人均增速仅 1.6%，结构性约束继续阻碍宏观成果向民生的传导。

1.1.2 几内亚增长加速：铝土矿驱动 + 服务业复苏

Guinea's mining-led growth has sustained strong aggregate performance but has consistently failed to translate into broad-based job creation or meaningful poverty reduction. From 2016 to 2024, GDP growth averaged 6.5 percent (3.8 percent per capita), driven primarily by the mining sector. With a median age of 17 and nearly 80 percent of the population under 35, Guinea faces a jobs challenge of generational proportions. Real effective exchange rate appreciation driven by the mining boom tends to erode the competitiveness of non-mining tradable sectors.

几内亚矿业驱动的增长保持了强劲的总量表现，但始终未能转化为广泛的就业创造或有意义的减贫。2016–2024 年 GDP 年均增长 6.5%（人均 3.8%），主要由矿业驱动，服务业（主要是商业）与农业（作物产量恢复）亦有贡献。几内亚年龄中位数仅 17 岁、近 80% 人口在 35 岁以下，就业挑战堪称"世代级"。矿业繁荣推动的实际有效汇率升值侵蚀非矿可贸易部门竞争力，妨碍多元化、就业与减贫。

Mining production was the primary driver of the growth acceleration in 2025. The mining sector grew by an estimated 13.9 percent, fueled by a surge in bauxite production—from 135 million tons in 2024 to 175 million tons in 2025, an increase of 30 percent—reflecting both higher capacity utilization by existing operators and the opening of two new mines. Gold prices rose sharply by 44 percent to US$3,442 per troy ounce, while aluminum prices increased by 9 percent. Despite high prices, gold export volumes fell by 6 percent to 69,746 kilograms driven by a three-month rainy season suspension and strengthened border security. The launch of Simandou operations in November 2025 marked a major milestone, with initial production reaching 1.5 million tons and exports totaling 1 million tons of iron ore in 2025.

矿业是 2025 年增速提升的首要驱动：矿业增长约 13.9%，铝土矿产量从 2024 年的 1.35 亿吨增至 2025 年的 1.75 亿吨（+30%），既因现有运营商产能利用率提升，也因两座新矿投产。金价大涨 44% 至每盎司 3442 美元，铝价上涨 9%；但黄金出口量下降 6% 至 69746 公斤（雨季三个月暂停作业+边境管控强化）。2025 年 11 月西芒杜投产是重大里程碑，当年即产出 150 万吨、出口 100 万吨铁矿石，此后三年逐步爬坡。

The non-mining economy expanded by an estimated 5.5 percent, enabled by more stable electricity supply in urban areas, private investment and employment linked to the final construction phase of Simandou infrastructure, higher public investment, public-sector wage revaluation spillovers, and election-related spending. However, Simandou-related investments remained highly capital- and import-intensive, with real imports increasing by 20.0 percent in 2025. Services recovered from 3.8 percent growth in 2024 to 5.4 percent in 2025; agriculture grew by 5.8 percent, below the 6.5 percent of 2024, reflecting weak fisheries and reduced market access as seasonal rains damaged road infrastructure.

非矿经济增长约 5.5%，得益于城市供电趋稳、西芒杜基建收尾阶段带动的私人投资与就业、公共投资加码、公务员工资调整的溢出效应及选举相关支出。但西芒杜相关投资高度资本与进口密集，2025 年实际进口增长 20%，净出口拖累增长。服务业从 2024 年的 3.8% 回升至 5.4%（商业、运输、公共行政、金融服务改善）；农业增长 5.8%（低于 2024 年的 6.5%），受渔业疲软和雨季损毁道路影响。

1.1.3 通胀大幅回落，但食品价格仍是顽疾

Headline inflation declined sharply from 5.1 percent in 2024 to 3.2 percent in 2025, driven by the fading impact of the December 2023 fuel depot explosion, normalization of transport and energy prices, and subdued import price pressures. Year-on-year inflation reached a low of 2.3 percent in April 2025 before rising to 3.7 percent by December as food price pressures re-emerged. The decrease in transport prices was the primary driver. Food inflation decelerated from 8.3 percent to 6.8 percent but remained persistent, contributing an estimated 2.7 percentage points to the 3.2 percent headline rate—reflecting structurally high food transport costs, low agricultural productivity, and recurrent weather shocks. Mamou recorded the lowest rate at 1.6 percent, while N'Zérékoré registered the highest at 4.6 percent and Conakry 3.6 percent. Severe food insecurity already affected an estimated 50 percent of the population in 2024.

整体通胀从 2024 年的 5.1% 大幅降至 2025 年的 3.2%，驱动力是 2023 年 12 月油库爆炸影响消退、运输与能源价格正常化、进口价格压力缓和。2025 年 4 月同比通胀低至 2.3%，12 月随食品价格压力重现回升至 3.7%。运输价格下降是主要降温因素；食品通胀从 8.3% 降至 6.8% 但依然顽固，为整体通胀贡献约 2.7 个百分点——背后是产销区距离遥远、农业生产率低和反复的天气冲击。分地区看，马木最低（1.6%），恩泽雷科雷最高（4.6%），科纳克里次之（3.6%）。2024 年全国约 50% 人口已处于严重粮食不安全状态。

1.1.4 贫困随增长与降温而下降，但结构性障碍仍在

Following a 4.2 percentage points increase between 2019-22, the national poverty rate is estimated to have fallen from 46.6 percent in 2023 to 43.3 percent in 2024 and 36.8 percent in 2025. The international poverty rate (US$4.20/day, 2021 PPP) dropped to 21.5 percent in 2025 from 26.7 percent in 2024. Preliminary results from the 2025 population census indicate Guinea's population has reached 17.5 million—around 20 percent higher than previously estimated—with a median age of just 17. The INS estimates average annual natural population growth of 3.1 percent between 1996 and 2025. Poverty remains predominantly rural. Rapid urbanization has increased density—particularly in Conakry—but without commensurate growth in formal wage employment.

全国贫困率在 2019–22 年上升 4.2 个百分点后，估计从 2023 年的 46.6% 降至 2024 年 43.3%、2025 年 36.8%；国际贫困线（4.20 美元/天）口径从 26.7% 降至 21.5%。2025 年人口普查初步结果显示几内亚人口已达 1750 万——比此前估计高约 20%——年龄中位数仅 17 岁；国家统计局估计 1996–2025 年人口自然增长率年均 3.1%。贫困仍以农村为主；快速城市化抬高了密度（尤其科纳克里），但正规工资就业并未同步增长。（注：贫困估计依赖微观模拟，因最新全国住户调查止于 2018 年。）

1.1.5 货币宽松支撑稳定，但金融部门约束限制信贷与现金供应

Following rate cuts in December 2024 and March 2025, the BCRG reduced the policy rate and reserve requirement ratio by 50 basis points in September 2025, to 9.75 and 11.75 percent respectively, and again in March 2026, to 9.50 and 11.50 percent. The average lending rate on new loans declined slightly to 13.82 percent in Q3 2025, still high. The nominal exchange rate remained stable at approximately GNF 8,638 per US dollar. The banking sector is liquid in electronic money but experiences scarcity of physical cash: bank vault holdings contracted 16.5 percent year-on-year; currency in circulation surged 35.1 percent, reflecting precautionary hoarding and physical settlement of gold purchases. Domestic credit increased 27 percent y/y in November 2025, but private sector credit remains low at 10.2 percent of GDP, the credit-to-deposit ratio only about 37 percent, and the NPL ratio rose to 8.3 percent—well above the 5 percent international benchmark.

继 2024 年 12 月和 2025 年 3 月降息后，几内亚央行（BCRG）2025 年 9 月将政策利率和存款准备金率各降 50 个基点至 9.75% 和 11.75%，2026 年 3 月再降至 9.50% 和 11.50%。新贷款平均利率小幅降至 13.82%（仍偏高）。名义汇率稳定在约 1 美元兑 8638 几郎。银行体系"电子货币充裕、实物现金短缺"：库存现金同比收缩 16.5%，流通货币激增 35.1%（预防性囤现+黄金采购以现钞结算所致），多家银行维持取现限额。2025 年 11 月国内信贷名义增长 27%，但私营部门信贷仅占 GDP 10.2%，贷存比仅约 37%，不良贷款率升至 8.3%（远高于 5% 的国际参考线）。

1.1.6 财政显著恶化：选举与资本开支扩大赤字，国内债务累积

The overall deficit widened to 8.9 percent of GDP from 4.9 percent in 2024. Total revenues increased sharply to 18.2 percent of GDP from 15.2 percent—the highest in over 20 years—with tax revenues rising from 13.2 to 16.3 percent of GDP. Mining revenues rose to 3.0 percent of GDP from 2.3 percent, reflecting higher bauxite production, strong prices, and improved compliance linked to the bauxite reference price mechanism. Total expenditure rose to 27.1 percent of GDP from 20.0 percent: government capital spending increased to 11.8 percent of GDP from 8.1 percent, while election-related expenses pushed goods and services outlays to 4.8 percent of GDP—more than double the previous year. Interest payments edged up from 1.2 to 1.5 percent of GDP.

财政总赤字从 2024 年占 GDP 4.9% 扩大至 8.9%。总收入从 15.2% 大幅升至 18.2%（20 余年最高），税收从 13.2% 升至 16.3%；矿业收入从 2.3% 升至 3.0%（铝土矿增产、价格坚挺、铝土矿参考价机制逐步落地改善合规）。总支出从 20.0% 升至 27.1%：政府资本开支从 8.1% 升至 11.8%，选举相关开支使货物与服务支出翻倍至 4.8%，利息支出从 1.2% 升至 1.5%。（注：上述比率基于重估前 GDP 序列；GDP 重估后各比率约低 5 个百分点，但不改变主要结论。）

1.1.7 投资热潮维持外部压力，但储备积累改善

The current account deficit held broadly flat at an estimated 14.5 percent of GDP in 2025 (14.3 percent in 2024), as import growth—driven by capital goods and petroleum imports for the final phase of Simandou construction—more than offset export gains. FDI remained the primary external financing source at an estimated 13.8 percent of GDP. Gross international reserves rebounded from a critically low US$1.4 billion (1.4 months of import cover) at end-2024 to US$3.7 billion (3.6 months) by end-2025, reflecting higher export receipts, the BCRG's gold operations program, and improved repatriation of export earnings. However, Guinea's real effective exchange rate (REER) appreciated approximately 8 percent in 2024, continuing a rise of around 54 percent since 2020—eroding the competitiveness of non-mining tradable sectors.

2025 年经常账户赤字估计为 GDP 的 14.5%（2024 年为 14.3%），西芒杜建设收尾阶段的资本品与成品油进口激增抵消了出口增长。外商直接投资（FDI）仍是主要外部融资来源，估计占 GDP 的 13.8%（略低于 2024 年的 14.9%，反映铁路港口基建接近尾声）。国际储备从 2024 年底 14 亿美元（仅 1.4 个月进口覆盖）的危急低点回升至 2025 年底 37 亿美元（3.6 个月），得益于出口收入增加、央行黄金收购计划和出口收入汇回改善。但矿业外汇流入持续推高实际有效汇率：2024 年升值约 8%，2020 年以来累计升值约 54%——持续侵蚀非矿可贸易部门的国际竞争力。

1.2 前景与风险 Outlook and Risks

1.2.1 全球不确定性上升，就业创造不足

According to the June 2026 Global Economic Prospects, global growth is projected to slow to 2.4 percent in 2026 before firming to 2.8 percent in 2027–28, shaped by the Middle East conflict and persistent trade policy uncertainty. In Sub-Saharan Africa, growth is projected at 4.1 percent in 2026, rising to 4.4 percent in 2027 and 4.5 percent in 2028—still too low to reduce poverty or create jobs at scale, with more than 620 million people entering the labor force by 2050.

据 2026 年 6 月《全球经济展望》，受中东冲突与贸易政策不确定性影响，全球增速预计 2026 年放缓至 2.4%，2027–28 年回升至 2.8%。发达经济体 2026 年仅增 1.6%；新兴市场与发展中经济体 2026 年放缓至 3.6%。撒南非洲 2026 年预计增长 4.1%、2027 年 4.4%、2028 年 4.5%——但仍不足以大规模减贫或创造就业：到 2050 年该地区将有超过 6.2 亿人进入劳动力市场。

1.2.2 西芒杜将把几内亚推入高增长期，但若不改革将带来就业与竞争力风险

After accelerating to 7.4 percent in 2025, growth is projected to surge to 8.8 percent in 2026, 11.1 percent in 2027, and 10.7 percent in 2028, averaging 10.3 percent over 2026–28—among the highest rates in the world (7.7 percent per capita). This outlook is driven overwhelmingly by mining, projected to grow 20.2 percent in 2026 and average 24.9 percent in 2027–28 as Simandou exports ramp up. After the ramp-up, growth would return to a 5-to-7 percent range depending on the extent of reforms. Non-mining growth is expected to dip slightly to 5.2 percent in 2026 before recovering to 5.9 percent in 2027–28.

在 2025 年提速至 7.4% 后，几内亚增速预计 2026 年达 8.8%、2027 年 11.1%、2028 年 10.7%，2026–28 年均 10.3%——位居全球最高之列（人均 7.7%）。这一前景几乎全部由矿业主导：矿业 2026 年预计增长 20.2%、2027–28 年均 24.9%。爬坡完成后，增速将回落至 5–7% 区间，取决于改革程度。非矿增长预计 2026 年小幅回落至 5.2%（高油价压制购买力），2027–28 年回升至 5.9%。

Agriculture is projected to grow at an average of 6.3 percent over 2026–28, benefiting from improved transport connectivity, including the planned opening of the Simandou railway corridor to commercial freight in 2027. The sector stands to benefit further from the Simandou 2040 program—a fifteen-year strategic framework endorsed by parliament in March 2026, organized around five pillars (agriculture and agro-industry; infrastructure and technology; education and culture; economy and finance; health and social well-being). The plan's first phase (2025–2030) envisages approximately US$65 billion in investment through 2030 (including US$20 billion for Simandou infrastructure), part of a total US$200 billion through 2040, of which 70 percent is expected from the private sector, supported by 36 structural reforms and a Guinea sovereign fund.

农业 2026–28 年预计年均增长 6.3%，受益于交通改善——包括 2027 年西芒杜铁路走廊计划向商业货运开放。"西芒杜 2040"计划于 2026 年 3 月获议会批准，是十五年战略框架，围绕五大支柱（农业与涉农工业；基础设施与技术；教育与文化；经济与金融；卫生与社会福祉）。首期（2025–2030）规划投资约 650 亿美元（含西芒杜基建 200 亿美元），至 2040 年总规模 2000 亿美元，其中 70% 预计来自私营部门，配套 36 项结构性改革和几内亚主权基金。

The conflict is expected to have a mixed but slightly negative impact, transmitting through three channels: commodity prices, shipping and freight costs, and global growth slowdown. GDP growth for 2026 has been revised down by 0.5 percentage points to 8.8 percent (from 9.3 percent pre-conflict), and inflation revised up 1 point to 6.2 percent. The national poverty rate is projected to decline to 32.0 percent in 2026, 27.9 percent in 2027, and 23.9 percent by 2028. However, rapid population growth means the number of poor people will only slowly decrease, remaining above 3.8 million by 2028. The fiscal deficit is projected to narrow gradually to 4.5 percent by 2028 as Simandou mining revenues ramp up; tax revenues are projected to reach around 17 percent of GDP by 2028, with mining taxes rising from 3.0 to 3.8 percent. Total spending is projected to ease from 27.1 to 23.4 percent of GDP in 2028, with capital expenditures maintained at around 11 percent. The debt-to-GDP ratio is projected to decline only marginally from 51.5 to 50.9 percent in 2028.

贫困率预计 2026 年降至 32.0%、2027 年 27.9%、2028 年 23.9%；但人口快速增长意味着贫困人口绝对数仅缓慢下降，2028 年仍在 380 万人以上。财政赤字预计逐步收窄至 2028 年的 4.5%；税收预计 2028 年达 GDP 的 17% 左右，矿业税从 3.0% 升至 3.8%（部分来自西芒杜铁路基建相关税收）。总支出预计从 27.1% 回落至 23.4%，资本开支维持约 11%。债务/GDP 比率预计仅从 51.5% 微降至 50.9%。

On the upside: a faster Simandou ramp-up, stronger commodity prices, and accelerated Simandou 2040 reforms.

1.2.3 西芒杜机遇窗口有限，政策行动刻不容缓

Beyond the medium term, Guinea's growth trajectory will depend critically on whether the mining boom translates into broader structural transformation. Under a Business-as-Usual scenario, growth settles at around 5.3 percent by 2030 and 5.1 percent by 2040, narrowly based and without meaningful job creation. Under a reform scenario, long-term growth could be 1.1 percentage points higher by 2030 and 1.8 points higher by 2040—reaching nearly 6.9 percent—with real GDP per capita 20 percent above the BAU path. The choice is not determined by Simandou itself, but by policy decisions in the years ahead.

中长期看，几内亚的增长轨迹取决于矿业繁荣能否转化为结构转型。"照旧情景"下增速 2030 年回落至约 5.3%、2040 年约 5.1%，基础狭窄、就业无实质改善；"改革情景"下 2030 年高 1.1 个百分点、2040 年高 1.8 个百分点（接近 6.9%），人均实际 GDP 比照旧路径高 20%。两条路径的分野不由西芒杜决定，而由未来几年的政策选择决定。

优先改革方向（第一章政策要点）：

  • 强化中期财政框架，把拟设的几内亚主权基金（GSF）锚定其中：发展自然资源收入预测系统；采用非资源初级平衡（NRPB）等明确财政锚；GSF 初期以"平抑收入波动"的稳定功能为先，治理遵循圣地亚哥原则并接受议会对年度审计报告的审议；
  • 维护债务可持续性：2024 年 DSA 的"中等风险"评级实为模型信号为高风险后经判断调整而来——更需财政纪律；新借款最大化优惠性；落实债务管理机构审计改革；按 2024 年部长令在线公开债务数据；
  • 强化创收与支出效率：重点治理矿业、银行、电信业的转让定价；电子报税扩围；财政-海关-矿业-国库数据互联；逐步改革不成比例流向富裕家庭的电力补贴（落实 2026 年电价调整、居民预付费电表全覆盖）；
  • 解决现金短缺、重建银行信任：央行全面诊断现金短缺成因；加快移动支付与数字支付基础设施（尤其城乡接合部与农村）；
  • 用西芒杜财政收入与走廊基建缩小空间贫困、扩大妇女机会：以空间贫困地图引导公共投资投向落后地区；将部分西芒杜财政收入配置于社会保障、卫生与教育；制度化更频繁的住户调查。

第二章 促进非矿业部门增长与就业 Chapter 2. Boosting Non-Mining Sector Growth and Jobs

**本章要点 Overview：**几内亚矿业驱动的增长带来的就业与减贫有限，暴露出总量经济表现与家庭福祉之间的结构性脱节。多数几内亚人从事非正规、低生产率工作，"有工作"几乎不能防贫——有工作与无工作家庭的贫困率相差不足两个百分点。妇女在劳动参与、资产拥有和融资方面面临叠加劣势。非矿私营部门虽有潜在活力，但受制于基础设施缺口、企业管理能力薄弱与融资不畅。把握西芒杜机遇需要：定向的生产率提升型基建投资、加强本地化政策执行以培育本地企业能力与融资、投资技能与教育、通过改善市场连接释放农业就业潜力。

本章将评估劳动力市场与私营部门动态，识别制约非矿企业的关键约束，评估近期改革（尤其是本地化政策）的有效性，并探讨市场化农业与涉农产业作为就业与多元化引擎的潜力。

2.1 背景——增长只带来微弱减贫 Context—Growth with Only Marginal Poverty Reduction

Guinea, entering its second mining boom within a decade, aims to break from the pattern of the first: economic growth without broad-based inclusion. Household survey data (EHCVM 2018) show that poverty rates differ little by employment status: 43.9 percent of individuals in households with an employed head are poor, compared to 42.0 percent in households with an unemployed head. Over 81 percent of Guineans live in households reliant on self-employment or family enterprises, where poverty incidence approaches 49 percent, versus 25.5 percent among wage-earner households. Job quality—notably wage-based employment—rather than job quantity alone, is central to poverty reduction.

几内亚在十年内迎来第二轮矿业繁荣，目标是打破第一轮的模式——有增长、无包容。住户调查（EHCVM 2018）显示贫困率与就业状态几乎无关：户主就业的家庭贫困率 43.9%，户主失业的 42.0%。逾 81% 的几内亚人生活在依赖自雇/家庭经营的家庭，贫困发生率近 49%，而工资雇员家庭为 25.5%。这说明就业质量（是否有工资性岗位）而非单纯的就业数量，才是减贫的核心。

2.2 劳动力市场动态 Labor Market Dynamics

2.2.1 劳动力市场特征与生产率提升

Increased investment since 2015 enabled capital deepening and gains in total factor productivity yet yielded limited job creation or poverty reduction. Guinea's first growth acceleration (2015–2024, real GDP averaging 6.2 percent vs 3.8 percent in 2005–2014) stemmed from the bauxite and gold boom. Yet 62 percent of the population and 80 percent of the poor live in rural areas. Growth accounting shows capital accumulation contributed an average 3.5 percentage points of GDP growth (vs 2.6 in 2005–2014), with modest labor contribution and a doubling of the TFP residual. More than two-thirds of productivity gains came from within-sector effects rather than labor reallocation.

2015 年以来的投资增长带来了资本深化与全要素生产率提升，但就业与减贫有限。第一轮提速（2015–2024 年实际 GDP 年均 6.2%，此前十年为 3.8%）源于铝土矿与黄金繁荣；但 62% 人口和 80% 贫困人口在农村。增长核算显示：资本积累平均贡献 3.5 个百分点（此前 2.6），劳动贡献有限，全要素生产率贡献翻倍。三分之二以上的生产率提升来自部门内部效应，而非劳动力跨部门再配置。

Declining female labor force participation has become macro-critical: it stands at 43 percent (down from 56.3 percent in 2018), compared to 63 percent for men (down from 76 percent). Women spend an average of 15.4 hours per week on unpaid care work, nearly three times men's 5.2 hours. Over 95 percent of female employment is classified as vulnerable (83 percent for men). Labor force indicators are weak relative to peers; the low unemployment rate likely reflects high informality and underemployment, and the shift toward rural, agriculture-dominated and artisanal gold mining activities.

女性劳动参与率下降已成为宏观层面的关键问题：目前仅 43%（2018 年为 56.3%），男性 63%（2018 年 76%）。女性每周无酬照护劳动平均 15.4 小时，接近男性（5.2 小时）的三倍；超过 95% 的女性就业为脆弱就业（男性 83%）。与同类国家相比，几内亚劳动参与率与就业率偏低；表面上的低失业率实际反映高非正规就业与就业不足，就业结构转向农村、农业主导及手工采金活动。

2.2.2 改善劳动力市场前景与就业增长

Employment grew overall between 2015 and 2022, with a modest increase in wage employment and a rising share of medium-skilled work. Agriculture's share has gradually declined, mirrored by a gradual increase in wage employment—consistent with the correlation between wage employment and GDP growth. Construction employment grew fastest, followed by mining, though both account for very small shares. Guinea exhibits an above-average share of employment in agriculture and a correspondingly lower share in manufacturing. The labor market has shifted from medium- to low-skilled employment in recent decades, while high-skilled employment accounts for less than 8 percent of total jobs.

2015–2022 年就业总体增长，工资性就业小幅增加。农业就业占比逐步下降，工资就业占比逐步上升（与 GDP 增长正相关）。建筑业就业增长最快、其次是矿业，但两者在总就业中占比都很小。与同类国家相比，几内亚农业就业占比偏高、制造业偏低，多数劳动者为自雇或家庭经营。近几十年劳动力市场出现从中技能向低技能岗位的下移，高技能岗位占比不足 8%——劳动力技能结构升级有限。

Guinea's rapidly expanding working-age population creates both an opportunity and an urgent need to expand productive employment. The working-age population (15–64) is projected to continue expanding between 2025 and 2034. While declining dependency ratios signal a potential demographic dividend, the level remains high compared to peers. Realizing this dividend depends on skills: deficits in foundational, technical, and job-relevant skills emerge early in the education system and accumulate over time, pointing to the need for stronger alignment between education and labor market needs and greater private sector engagement in skills development.

几内亚快速扩张的劳动年龄人口既是机遇也是紧迫挑战：15–64 岁人口将在 2025–2034 年持续扩张，抚养比下降预示人口红利窗口，但抚养比绝对水平仍高于同类国家。兑现红利取决于技能：基础性、技术性、岗位相关技能的缺口在教育早期就出现并逐年累积，大量劳动者在缺乏必要能力的情况下进入劳动力市场——需要教育与劳动力市场需求更好对接、机构间更好协调、私营部门更深参与技能培养。

2.3 非矿私营部门的就业与减贫潜力 The Non-Mining Private Sector Holds Potential

2.3.1 正规部门：有潜在活力但规模小、约束多

WBES firms reported strong annual growth in sales (7.7 percent) and employment (8.6 percent) during 2021–24, both above SSA and LMIC averages. Yet transport was the top obstacle for 17 percent of firms—approximately three times the SSA average—followed by customs and trade regulations (14 percent) and political instability (13 percent, vs ~8 percent SSA average). Transportation is especially acute for small and medium firms; small firms also cited customs and political stability among top-three constraints, while medium firms cited tax administration and labor regulations. Surprisingly, few firms identified access to finance or land as their biggest constraint—possibly reflecting deficiencies in financial service availability rather than adequacy. Only 3.9 percent of firms have a bank loan or line of credit, and 92 percent of investment is financed internally. The 2014 Labor Code remains in force with the minimum salary at GNF 550,000 since 2022; a comprehensive revision was finalized as a draft in January 2025 and awaits adoption. 79 percent of firms face competition from informal competitors (SSA average 56.4 percent), but fewer than 10 percent view it as their primary obstacle. Management practices trail regional norms: Guinea's management index scores 33 vs 47–48 in SSA lower-middle-income countries.

世界银行企业调查显示，几内亚正规企业 2021–24 年实际销售年均增长 7.7%、就业增长 8.6%，均高于撒南非洲和中低收入国家平均。但 17% 的企业把运输物流列为首要障碍（约为撒南平均的 3 倍），其次是海关与贸易管制（14%）和政治不稳定（13%，撒南平均约 8%）。运输约束对中小企业尤为尖锐。出乎意料的是，很少企业把融资或土地列为首要约束——这可能反映金融服务供给不足而非充裕：仅 3.9% 的企业有银行贷款或授信额度，92% 的投资靠内部资金。2014 年《劳动法》仍然有效，最低工资 2022 年以来固定为 55 万几郎；全面修订草案 2025 年 1 月已定稿、待过渡议会通过。79% 的企业面临非正规竞争者（撒南平均 56.4%）。企业管理指数得分 33，低于撒南中低收入国家的 47–48。

2.3.2 更广义的非矿部门：非正规、能力受限、缺乏金融渠道

The broader non-mining private sector is overwhelmingly informal, with an estimated 92 percent of private businesses operating as micro-enterprises. The informal economy accounts for approximately 40 percent of GDP. Vulnerable employment accounts for 79.2 percent of total employment. Exports from Simandou—the world's largest iron ore deposit—began in December 2025, positioning Guinea to mobilize mining revenues in support of economic diversification and better jobs.

更广义的非矿私营部门压倒性地非正规：约 92% 的私营企业是微型企业，非正规经济约占 GDP 的 40%。脆弱就业占总就业的 79.2%。2025 年 12 月，全球最大铁矿床西芒杜开始出口，使几内亚有能力动员矿业收入支持经济多元化与更好的就业。

**Simandou 2040 计划目标与就业现实：**西芒杜满产可拉动全国 GDP 增长约 25–40%，但直接与间接就业仅预计 1.5–1.8 万个——远远不足以吸收快速增长的劳动力（79% 在 35 岁以下）。贫困的空间分布显示高贫困区远不限于走廊沿线，需要面向落后地区的再分配与公共投资。2025 年 2 月通过、8 月实施的矿业新集体协议引入了重要改善：附加福利（退休、家庭假等）、分包监管框架（分包工人工资不得低于发包方同岗位的 70%）、最低工资 260 万几郎的薪酬表，并适用于全行业所有运营商。

2.3.3 强化本地化政策以促进非矿增长与就业

One entry point to fostering non-mining growth is to leverage mining sector growth through local content requirements embedded in most agreements with mining companies. A local content approach targets sales from domestic firms to a fast-growing domestic sub-sector (mining represents around 12 percent of GDP in 2025), comparable to the export-led approach successfully implemented in East Asia. Guinea's main local content law (Loi L/2022/010/CNT) mandates that businesses, especially in mining and energy, employ a minimum percentage of local workers across managerial and operational roles. A subsequent law (L/2024/013/CNT) established the Autorité de Régulation du Contenu Local. Decree 2024/278 clarified the Authority's mandate; Decree D/2025/074/PRG/CNRD/SGG clarified the Ministry of Mines' role. Guinea's revocation in 2025 of several mining permits for non-compliance with local content regulations signaled enforcement commitment.

培育非矿增长的一个切入点，是利用嵌入多数矿业协议的本地内容要求，鼓励矿业采购决策帮助本地企业成长。本地化路径瞄准本土企业对快速增长的本国矿业（2025 年约占 GDP 的 12%）的销售，可比照东亚成功的出口导向路径。几内亚主要的本地化法（L/2022/010/CNT）强制矿业与能源企业在管理与操作岗位雇用最低比例本地人；后续法律（L/2024/013/CNT）设立本地化监管局；2024/278 号法令明确该局职责，D/2025/074 号法令明确矿业地质部的角色。2025 年几内亚曾因企业不遵守本地化规定而吊销数个矿业许可，展示了执法决心。

Businesses supplying Simandou could yield nascent improvements in local standards, capacity and jobs, yet impact rests on effective implementation. The impact would be limited initially, circumscribed by the size of Simandou procurement opportunities (estimated conservatively at 0.5–1.0 percent of GDP) and firms' capacity constraints. Effective local content policies typically encompass four elements: (i) participation of nationals in ownership, management, and employment; (ii) local sourcing of raw materials or value-added components; (iii) a clear geographic definition of "local" firm; (iv) distinctions by enterprise size.

向西芒杜供货可带动本地标准、能力与就业的初步改善，但影响取决于本地化政策与改革的有效执行。初期影响有限——西芒杜矿业采购规模保守估计仅为 GDP 的 0.5–1.0%，且本地企业能力受限。有效的本地化政策通常含四要素：①本国人参与所有权、管理与就业；②本地采购原材料或增值部件；③明确"本地"企业的地理定义；④按企业规模区分待遇。

Guinea's supplier base remains thin: few local firms can consistently provide goods and services meeting mining companies' quality, quantity and cost requirements, and Guinea faces a shortage of needed skills (Rio Tinto and Simfer, 2026). The BSTP provides an information-sharing platform matching mining procurement with suppliers, but its capacity is limited. Under Simandou 2040, the authorities established (March 2026) the Guinea Development Board (GDB) as a one-stop shop for private investment. A recent study of local mining contractors found infrastructure deficits—poor roads and unreliable electricity outside Conakry—were the most acute impediment to competitiveness; access to affordable finance was the second structural barrier. Local firms also struggle with competitive tendering procedures whose eligibility criteria and administrative burdens systematically disadvantage smaller domestic firms; training institutes remain concentrated in Conakry or the Boké bauxite region, mismatched with Simandou corridor labor demand; in areas such as Beyla, informal competition undercuts labor and safety standards; 40 percent of local firms report annual turnover below US$50,000.

几内亚供应商基础薄弱：很少有本地企业能持续满足矿业公司的质量、数量与成本要求，技能短缺明显（力拓与 SimFer，2026）。BSTP 提供采购撮合平台但能力有限。2026 年 3 月，几内亚在 2040 计划下成立**几内亚发展委员会（GDB）**作为私人投资一站式窗口。一项对矿业本地承包商的调查发现：基础设施缺陷（科纳克里以外路况差、电力不可靠）是竞争力的头号障碍，可负担融资次之；招标程序的资质门槛与行政负担系统性不利于本地小企业；培训机构集中在科纳克里或博凯铝土矿区，与西芒杜走廊用工需求地理错配；贝拉等地非正规企业压低劳工与安全标准；40% 的本地企业年营业额不足 5 万美元。

政策建议（西非经验）：① 设立专职本地化机构协调供应商发展计划（SDP）；② 通过专项基金/矿业公司/发展伙伴保障供应商可负担融资；③ 要求矿业公司披露本地采购与 SDP 参与情况；④ 发展援助方公开报告成果；⑤ 以 WTO 合规的"日落条款"协调双边贸易协定；⑥ 区域协作共享供应商能力信息。报告指出西芒杜本地化政策需更新——首版 2015 年由力拓按单一矿山设想发布，如今共享铁路港口已成为核心。能力建设应包括培训、技术转移、与国际公司组建合资/联营，并在特定行业培育"本地冠军企业"。

**金融约束：**几内亚私营部门信贷水平为撒南最低之列；仅 23% 人口有银行账户；贷款利率超 11%；银行资产负债表仅为 GDP 的 26.5%（塞内加尔 66.5%），保险渗透率仅 0.5%。央行高再贴现率与单客户放款限额约束银行支持本地企业。中小企业加速器计划（力拓几内亚与 APIP 共创）首期 10 家企业试点已完成，2026 年 3 月起由 GDB 接手筹备第二期。

2.4 扩展市场化农业与涉农价值链 Expanding Market-Oriented Agriculture and Agri-Business Value Chains

Agriculture comprises 31 percent of Guinea's GDP and over 60 percent of employment (2019), making it the most employment-intensive sector. Yet agricultural labor productivity remains significantly lower than other sectors, with a decade-long TFP stagnation. Output growth has been driven by land-extensive expansion rather than productivity gains. Post-harvest losses remain substantial: 10 percent for rice, 15–20 percent for maize, 20–45 percent for roots and tubers, and 30–60 percent for fruits and vegetables. About one third of farmers live more than two hours from the nearest urban market.

农业占几内亚 GDP 的 31%、就业的 60% 以上（2019 年），是就业密度最高的部门。但农业劳动生产率显著低于其他部门，全要素生产率停滞了十年，产出增长主要靠扩大耕地而非效率提升。采后损失惊人：水稻 10%、玉米 15–20%、块根块茎 20–45%、果蔬 30–60%。约三分之一农户距最近城镇市场超过两小时，路况差、冷链缺电、集散平台不足使国产大米在物流失灵时被廉价进口米挤占。

Niche value chains—fonio, rice, mango, pineapple, fish, cashew—show promising momentum. The Simandou corridor could reduce unit costs for bulk inputs and refrigerated shipments, but realizing benefits depends on corridor governance: finalized multi-user rail-port access regimes with equitable pricing and slot allocation. The BSTP online marketplace channeled US$73.6 million in mining procurement by November 2023, rising to US$236.5 million by October 2024, with thousands of SME representatives trained and rising participation by women-owned firms—opening procurement channels for agri-SMEs in catering, food supply and logistics linked to mining camps.

福尼奥米、大米、芒果、菠萝、水产、腰果等利基价值链势头良好。西芒杜走廊若实现多用户公平接入（定价规则、时段分配），可降低化肥等大宗投入品与冷藏运输的单位成本。BSTP 线上平台撮合的矿业采购从 2023 年 11 月的 7360 万美元增至 2024 年 10 月的 2.365 亿美元，数千名 SME 代表受训，女性所有企业参与度上升——为涉农 SME 打开了营地餐饮、食品供应与物流的采购渠道。
